<img src="logo.png" alt="Slideshow icon: a framed sunset photo of blue hills, with an orange play button at its corner, on a deep blue background" width="128">

## A simple slideshow app to display photos/images full-screen

Emphasis is on **_simplicity_**. Give it a folder of images, and it displays them full-screen, one at a time.

## Requirements
- macOS: 15 (Sequoia)
